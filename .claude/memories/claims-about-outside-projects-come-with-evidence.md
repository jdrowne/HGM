# Claims about outside projects come with evidence

A statement about how another project works, or what its authors intended, is given together with what it rests on: a quoted line from that project's own documentation or code, or a check run on the local copy. When no evidence is found, the statement is labelled as an inference.

**Why:** a confident sentence with nothing behind it cannot be checked, and decisions about the benchmark get made on such sentences.

**Scope (my interpretation):** any claim about an outside project, not only the benchmark.

**How to apply:**

- Fetch the primary source before answering, quote its exact words, and name the file they come from in the sentence itself. No list of links at the end, per [No Sources lists](no-sources-lists.md).
- Say plainly where an earlier claim was too strong or could not be backed up.
