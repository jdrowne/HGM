# A decision goes into the issue's body and title

When a decision is made on an issue and work remains, rewrite the issue's body and title to describe that work, and remove the "needs decision" label. Do not record the decision as a comment, and do not leave the label on to hold the work back.

**Why:** The body and title are what whoever picks up the issue reads. A decision left in a comment leaves them describing a question that is already answered, and the label says a person still has to decide when nobody does.

**How to apply:** Replace a "Decide whether…" title with one that names the work. Fold the reasons and anything found in the code into the body. A comment already posted for the decision is deleted once the body holds it. Related: [The user's words are restated, never copied](user-words-are-never-recorded-verbatim.md).
